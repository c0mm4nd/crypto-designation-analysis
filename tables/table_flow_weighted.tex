\caption{\textbf{Removal effects weighted by value as well as by address count.} The component-share measure used elsewhere counts addresses, so it reports how many accounts lose their only route rather than how much value does. Here the same removals are scored three ways: the share of retained addresses in the largest weakly connected component, the share of transferred USDT on pairs whose endpoints both survive and remain in it, and the share of such pairs. Values are percentage losses relative to the intact network. Weighting by value narrows the gap between designated addresses and undesignated hubs everywhere and reverses it in the counter-terrorism network. These are crawled two-hop networks, in which no structural quantity is identified (Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:boundary}); they are given so that the measure's behaviour can be seen.}
