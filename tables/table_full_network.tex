Of the 490 addresses named in the seizure orders, 400 appear. The degree-matched row gives the mean and 2.5--97.5 percentile range over 200 draws, with the structural column giving the share of addresses whose whole neighbourhood is removed; the random row is the mean of three. Removing every designated address costs about the same as removing the same number of undesignated addresses of the same degree on the structural measure, where it sits at the 68th percentile of the draws, and more than all of them on throughput, which is what designation selected for. Throughput is value on pairs incident to the removed set and excludes the stranded value, which is reported separately in the last column. It strands 590 USDT between surviving addresses, whereas 400 undesignated addresses of the highest degree strand 3.4 billion, 0.022\% of all transferred value.}
